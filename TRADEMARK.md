## You may

- Say that your product, fork or service is "built on sprag", "compatible
  with sprag" or "a fork of sprag", as long as the statement is accurate.
- Use the name in articles, talks, tutorials, package descriptions and
  comparison tables when you are referring to this project.
- Keep the name in the `NOTICE` file and in copyright or attribution lines,
  which the license requires you to keep anyway.
- Redistribute unmodified releases under the name they were published with.

## You may not

- Call a fork, a modified build or a hosted service "sprag", or use a name
  that differs only in spelling, spacing, capitalisation or a suffix such as
  "sprag Cloud", "sprag Pro", "sprag Team" or "getsprag".
- Register a domain, a package name, a social-media handle or an app-store
  listing that contains "sprag" for a product that is not this project.
- Use the logo for anything other than linking to or referring to this
  project.
- Suggest that this project endorses, maintains or supports your product,
  or that your product is the official version.

## Forks

A fork is welcome; that is what the license is for. Please give it its own
name, its own logo and its own package name, so that users can tell which
project they are installing and where to file issues. A fork that keeps
the `sprag` command name for compatibility should still present itself
under its own project name in its README, package metadata and website.
