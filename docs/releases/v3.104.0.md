- **The delegation guard is on by default.** `sprag install` registers the PreToolUse hook and, the first time it does, says in one line what it appends to delegation prompts. The 2026-11-07 replay is the reason: a multi-file lookup sent to Sonnet without an effort was relayable at medium in 0 of 3 runs and at high in 2 of 3. An explicit `sprag delegate off` is kept across upgrades, and the Korean style section still follows the `korean on` setting.
- **What effort raises saved now shows as `Effort saved`.** A raised delegation is written to a ledger (`effort-raise-ledger.json`) instead of the scan cache, and the statusline gives it a line under the Doc2md line in the same shape as Routing saved: `🔬 Effort saved $12.4 since 11/1  |  medium→high ×2 $12.4`. The figure is the routing saving the raised runs produced, and the count sits in the breakdown as `medium→high ×N`; a raise no rule routed is counted as `no rule ×N (n/a)`, and with no measured saving at all only a `🔬↑N` count chip remains. `sprag saved` prints the same shape: `Effort saved: $12.40 · 2 raised · run cost $1.23`. The saving is what a delegation that would have relayed a wrong answer unchecked at medium saved by routing, so it is already part of Routing saved and is never added on top. The figure follows the same reset mark as Routing saved: `sprag saved reset routing` and the monthly reset apply at once instead of waiting for the next scan, and raises outside the scan window are no longer lost.
- **The team chip leads with the license and names the organization and what the rank measures.** `member-1e500474 🦊 Fox #3 of 12` never said a rank of what. It now reads `Pro member-1e500474 amazon overall score #3 of 12, team AWS-FSI #1 of 4 🦊 Fox`: the plan, the organization, the overall score the office page ranks by, and your place within your own team. The team rank counts the same score among teammates only, so it never disagrees with the organization rank, and a one-team organization says the number once. It needs the updated team server.
- **`sprag team status` says what moving up the standings takes, and shows the field guide.** It opens with how many people are ahead of you (or the crown at #1). The overall score adds the spend rank (62.5%) and the delegation-share rank (37.5%), so the spend rank rises with more tokens and the delegation rank rises by handing a larger share of the same spend to cheaper models; the six species from Wolf up also need a delegation share at or above the company median. The next species comes with the percentile it starts at, and the field guide lists all thirty species by tier with their starting percentiles and a ▶ on yours. Once the team server is updated, the block adds this machine's spend, delegation share, the company median and how many people stand between you and the next species.
- **A budget stated in the prompt replaces the guard's default cap.** An implementation hand-off that stated about 90 tool calls still got the rule's 6,100-token cap, and a t1-worker stopped after 7 calls.
- **The cases behind the proof page live in the repository as source.** `evals/` holds one JSON file per case, and one build produces the site's `/proof/`, the evidence table in the READMEs and the summary shipped in the package from that source. `sprag proof` prints the summary offline. The harvest, replay, blind grading and publish pipeline is in `scripts/evals/`, with a weekly digest that posts new delegation candidates to Slack every Monday.
- **The README, the package description and the site's first screen describe Sprag as the delegation layer.** For each task it picks the model and the effort, in both directions, with public replay evidence and one set of team rules shared by Pro and Enterprise.
