- **The team console now breaks savings down by team.** The /team overview and the /ops organization page get a savings card: savings for the range with the change from the previous period, the daily average, the first-half vs second-half daily trend, savings per spend and the top saving team. The chart shows savings per day, week or month stacked by team, or cumulative, and the table ranks teams by savings, share, change, trend, savings per member and delegations, with CSV export.
- **Clicking a member name copies it.** Names in the /team members table and in the seat tables of /team and /ops copy to the clipboard and show "Copied" for a moment.
- **A session open for more than a day gets a warning and a handoff.** Once a session is a day old, the briefing fires once per day: the model wraps up, fills a handoff with `sprag handoff`, and gives you the exact sentence, with the file path, to type in a new session. Separately, the delegation hints now judge only the request itself when a wrapper such as a Slack bot appends a "[운영 규칙]" paragraph to every message.
