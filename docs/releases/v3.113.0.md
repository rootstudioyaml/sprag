- **The team line now shows your species as a gauge and names the ranks behind it.** The statusline team line draws the overall score percentile as a gauge with the percentile the next species starts at (`🦊 Fox ▰▰▰▰▰▰▰▰▰▱▱▱ 75% → 🐆 Cheetah 77%`), and lists the overall, spend and delegation ranks the score is built from (`overall #3/12 · spend #2/12 · delegation #5/12`), so the score percentile is the only percentage on the line. `sprag team status` adds the same ranks next to spend and delegation share; an older server sends no axis ranks and the line shows the overall rank only.
