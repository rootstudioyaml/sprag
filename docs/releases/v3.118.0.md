- **Animal icons are now thumbnails of the real 3D characters.** The office legend strip, the field guide, the drawer header and the animal breakdown in the town drawer show thumbnails of all 31 cast members rendered in Blender; they come from one 233 KB atlas, the previous icons still appear if that file fails to load, and the render script lives in `tools/blender/render_thumbs.py`.
- **The office has new lighting and shadows.** Ambient light is lower and contact shadows under desks and chairs keep furniture on the floor; the sandy grain on character surfaces is softened, and the ghost for people without data is a pale see-through spirit instead of a grey lump.
- **The operator town flies the camera to the company you pick.** Clicking a building frames it together with the animals standing in front of it and closing the drawer glides back to the previous view; the animals are 1.5 times larger, the selection ring is a thin outline, and signboards dim below the lit windows at night.
