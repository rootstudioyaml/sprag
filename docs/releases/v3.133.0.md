- **Your Sprag Town animal now runs in the menu bar.** It is the one of thirty species that matches your town grade, and a ghost floats there when you have no team or no data yet. Instead of a husky that moved only its legs, the whole body moves: the back gathers and stretches, the body pitches and bounces, and the tail trails a beat behind. The face is turned toward you so both eyes show. Birds, the dragon and the unicorn fly with beating wings, the frog hops, and the duck and the chick walk on two legs.
- **A process left by an ended session can reopen that session.** Clicking its line in the dashboard opens the Claude session that started it with `claude --resume`, in a new terminal window in the folder it worked in. You can read what the session was doing before deciding whether to stop a service such as Docker, and once the session is back its processes count as its own again.
