- **Sprag Bar compiles on Macs with Swift 6.0.** The 255-line drawing of the menu bar animal sat in one closure inside `NSImage`, and Swift 6.0 type-checks such a closure together with the call around it, so it stopped with "unable to type-check this expression in reasonable time" and the menu bar app never started. The drawing now lives in its own function, a Swift 6.0 toolchain compiles the source, and every frame of all 31 animals draws exactly as before. ([#22](https://github.com/rootstudioyaml/sprag/issues/22))
- **The next session after upgrading compiles Sprag Bar again.** The source changed, so a recorded failure does not hold the build back for 24 hours.
