- **The CLI explains an organization whose term has ended.** Fourteen days after an organization's term ends the server stops accepting new uploads and joins; `sprag team status` and `sprag team join` then tell you to ask the organization owner to renew instead of printing the server's refusal. - **Organizations now have a term.** The operator console opens an organization, extends its term or adds seats in one click. /team tells owners and admins 14 days before the term ends, and uploads keep working through a 14-day grace period after it. Existing organizations have no term and keep working as before.
