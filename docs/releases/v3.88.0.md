- **The tiger, cheetah, eagle, panda, hedgehog, lion and elephant are reworked against reference images.** The tiger loses the vertical stripes that made its head look like a watermelon and gets a 王 mark on the forehead, two short stripes on each cheek, three arcs over the back of the head and rings on its forearms; its ears are orange with pale inner ears and a white spot on the back, and the sweatband that hid the forehead is gone. The cheetah's tear marks, which read as three black dashes beside the nose, are removed, and its spots now cover the sides and back of the head and the limbs. The eagle's beak is a down-curving hook instead of the cone that baked as a yellow ball, and the panda's eye patches have a cleaner rim. The hedgehog's rounded hairdo becomes a coat of sharp quills in alternating dark and light tones with a longer snout, the lion's mane is a deeper orange-brown with a third ring over the back of the head, and the elephant has larger ears with pink inner ears and a longer trunk. The seated and standing glb files of the seven species are replaced.
