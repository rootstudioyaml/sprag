Questions

How are seats counted?

Seats are bought per organization. A seat is one machine active in the last 30 days, and an admin can merge a person's second machine into the same seat. Joins past the limit are refused until Sprag raises it. To add seats, contact sales@sprag.io.

Is anything uploaded by default?

No. Nothing is uploaded until a member joins a team, and the free CLI never needs an account. sprag team status --show-payload prints exactly what a member sends.

Who consents when a company rolls this out?

For an organization deployment, the organization's decision counts as the consent. Members still see what is sent with sprag team status, and sprag team leave wins over the env var.

Who decides on prompt capture?

Only an organization owner or admin, and only on the Enterprise plan. It is off by default, and every member can see its state in sprag team status.

Can I switch plans or cancel?

Yes. Moving between Pro and Enterprise changes the per-seat price from the next billing period, and canceling keeps the free CLI working as before.

Where is the data stored?

In Supabase, in the ap-northeast-2 (Seoul) region.
