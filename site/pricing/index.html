Pricing

Sprag is free. Teams pay per seat.

The CLI on your machine costs nothing and stays on your machine. A team plan adds a shared view of spend and savings for whoever owns the bill.

Free

For anyone running Claude Code or Codex alone.

  • The open-source CLI
  • Local routing rules
  • Savings ledger
  • doc2md and the statusline

Nothing leaves your machine.

How seats are counted

A seat is a member who was active in the last 30 days. Members who left are not billed.

What is sent

Members send one row per day: spend, savings, delegation count, model-family share, sprag version. No prompts, transcripts, paths or project names unless prompt capture is on.

How billing starts

Create your team today. Billing is invoiced monthly per active seat and starts when we enable it for your team from the dashboard. You will see the switch there first.

Questions

Is anything uploaded by default?

No. Team features are opt-in. Nothing is uploaded until a member joins a team, and the free CLI never needs an account.

Who consents when a company rolls this out?

For an organization deployment, the organization's decision counts as the consent. Members still see what is sent with sprag team status.

Can a member leave?

Yes. A member can leave at any time, and the owner can remove a member from the dashboard. A member who left is no longer billed.

Who decides on prompt capture?

It is a per-team policy that only the owner can change, and every member can see it in sprag team status. It needs the Team Plus plan.

Where is the data stored?

In Supabase, in the ap-northeast-2 (Seoul) region.
