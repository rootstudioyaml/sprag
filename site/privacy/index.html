Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-11-14

This policy covers the paid team features at sprag.io and the orders for them.

Scope

  • The free Sprag CLI on its own sends nothing and needs no account. Every place the CLI can reach over the network is listed in the network access table.
  • This policy applies to the paid team features, the pages at sprag.io, and orders for the paid plans.

What we collect

  • Orders: organization name, owner and admin emails, the payer's name and email, and business details for a tax invoice (for bank deposit payments). Card numbers and other payment details are handled by the payment provider, and Sprag does not store them.
  • Sign-in: the owner or admin email.
  • Inquiry form: email, name, company or team name, expected number of people and the message you write. Name, company and number of people are optional.
  • Team sync, from computers that joined a team: display name, OS name and version, CPU architecture, Sprag version, daily totals of spend, savings and delegated runs, and the share of each model family. Host names, user names and file paths are not sent.
  • Prompt capture, only on Enterprise and only when the organization turns it on: for prompts a person typed themselves, the time, a per-machine alias instead of the project folder name, and the text (up to 20,000 characters, with secrets, personal data, network addresses, URLs and file paths masked).
  • Ad measurement: when you visit the public pages at sprag.io, Google Ads receives the page visited, your IP address, browser information and advertising cookie identifiers. If you copy the install command, click an email link or send the inquiry form, Google Ads also receives that action.

Why we use it

  • To take and fulfill orders, handle payment and issue tax invoices.
  • To open organizations, sign people in and show the team dashboard.
  • To answer questions and requests, including those sent through the inquiry form, and to meet legal duties.
  • To measure whether ads lead people to sprag.io.

How long we keep it

  • Organization data is deleted within 30 days after the organization is closed.
  • Inquiry form submissions are deleted 1 year after we receive them.
  • Contract and payment records are kept for 5 years, and consumer complaint and dispute records for 3 years, as the Act on Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce requires.
  • We do not sell personal data, and we share it only with the processors below or where the law requires.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, delete or stop the processing of your personal data. Write to support@sprag.io and we will handle the request without delay.

How we destroy data

When the retention period ends or the purpose is fulfilled, we delete the data in a way that cannot be restored. Records we must keep by law are kept separately until that period ends.

Cookies and similar tools

  • sprag.io loads the Google Ads tag (gtag.js) to measure whether ads lead people to sprag.io and whether those visitors copy the install command, click an email link or send the inquiry form. Google may set advertising cookies for this. The site sets no cookies of its own. Visits are also counted with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies.
  • Your browser's local storage keeps your language choice and, after you sign in at sprag.io/team, the sign-in session.
  • Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, and the dashboard pages load a script from jsDelivr, so those providers see your IP address as in any web request.
  • You can block these cookies in your browser settings or turn off ad personalization at adssettings.google.com. The site keeps working either way.

Security measures

  • Data is encrypted in transit.
  • Row-level access control in the database limits each person to the data of their own organization.
  • We collect only what the service needs.
